HOW I THINK ABOUT ARCHITECTURE

I start with what an organisation is trying to achieve and the constraints it needs to respect. I make choices, dependencies and trade-offs visible, then connect architecture principles to engineering practice. A design is useful when teams can build, operate, recover and change the system.
